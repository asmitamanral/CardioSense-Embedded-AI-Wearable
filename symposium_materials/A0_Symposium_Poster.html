3. Tri-Modular Sternal Pod Design

The chest node supports three interchangeable wear modes without wet adhesives:

  • Mode 1A — Direct Skin Adhesive Dock: Reusable silicone medical dock for vigorous exercise (cycling, gym). Dry 316L stainless steel ECG contacts; no hydrogel.
  • Mode 1B — Glue-Free Soft Sentry Cord: Elastic chest cord for elderly or sensitive skin. Zero adhesives, zero irritation.
  • Mode 2 — Casual Clothing Clip: Clips over shirts for daily errands. 30-second finger-touch ECG on-demand via exposed contacts.

Key Innovation: Dry stainless steel contacts are sanitized with 70% isopropyl alcohol wipes between hospital patients — enabling 6× reuse/year.

5. Edge AI & Motion-Gated Intelligence

Dual-Layer AI Architecture

  • Foundation Layer: CNN-LSTM pre-trained on >40,000 hospital cases for universal ischemic physics (PEP lengthening, AO recoil collapse, ST-segment drift).
  • Personalization Layer: On-device unsupervised Autoencoder adapts to each wearer's 24h resting baseline, eliminating individual false alarms.

Hybrid Motion Denoising Pipeline
