The 4-Min vs 20-Min Paradox: Irreversible anoxic brain death begins in 4 minutes of cardiac arrest. Indian ambulance response averages 20–40 minutes.

Why Hasn't This Been Solved?

  • Smartwatches: Detect pulse loss post-collapse — biologically futile. Blind to respiration.
  • Holter/ECG Patches: Wet hydrogel dries in 24–48h. Contact dermatitis. <15% long-term compliance.
  • ZOLL LifeVest: $3,500–$4,000/month rental. Bulky, impractical for daily life.

3 Pre-Arrest Warning Signs

Over 50% of cardiac events emit multi-system warnings 15–30 minutes before collapse:

[ Coronary Ischemia ] → Na⁺/K⁺ pump failure → Ventricular contractility drops │ ┌─────┴─────┐ ▼ ▼ CARDIAC: AUTONOMIC: • PEP >140ms • Cold sweat surge • AO drop>50% • GSR >+3.5 µS/min • ST drift • SpO₂ < 88% • HRV crash • Agonal gasping • ALL AT REST

Key Insight: These signals occur while the patient is physically still — distinguishing true crisis from exercise.

4 Tri-Modal Zero-Glue Mechanics

316L Surgical Stainless Steel Dry Electrodes

Same hypoallergenic alloy used in hip implants. No hydrogel. Sanitized between patients with 70% IPA wipes → 6× hospital reuse/year.

Three Interchangeable Wear Modes

  • Mode 1A — Adhesive Dock: Reusable medical silicone for vigorous exercise (gym, cycling).
  • Mode 1B — Soft Sentry Cord: Elastic chest cord for elderly or sensitive skin. Zero adhesives.
  • Mode 2 — Clothing Clip: Clips over shirts for daily errands. 30s finger-touch ECG on demand.

5 Edge AI & Motion Denoising

Dual-Layer AI Architecture

  • Foundation CNN-LSTM: Pre-trained on >40,000 hospital ICU cases for universal ischemic physics recognition.
  • Personal Autoencoder: Adapts to each wearer's 24-hour resting baseline on-device. Anomaly = reconstruction failure.

Hybrid Motion Denoising (NLMS + DWT)

Wrist IMU captures walking rhythm → algorithm subtracts footstrike shockwaves from chest cardiac signals (like noise-cancelling headphones for the heart).
