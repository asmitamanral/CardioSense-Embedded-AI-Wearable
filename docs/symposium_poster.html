The 4-Minute vs. 20-Minute Paradox: Biological irreversible brain death occurs in 4 minutes of circulatory collapse. However, Indian urban/rural ambulance transit averages 20–40 minutes.

Why Existing Technologies Fail:

  • Consumer Smartwatches: Detect pulse loss post-collapse (biologically futile); prone to pervasive exercise false alarms; blind to breathing.
  • Clinical Holter/ECG Patches: Wet hydrogel electrodes dry in 24–48 hours, cause contact dermatitis, and suffer from <15% long-term patient compliance.

2. Pre-Arrest Electrophysiology

Over 50% of out-of-hospital cardiac events exhibit multi-system precursor symptoms 15 to 30 minutes before cardiac arrest:

[ Acute Myocardial Ischemia (Na+/K+ pump failure) ] │ ▼ [ Stroke Volume Drop & MAP Depolarization ] │ ┌───────┴───────┐ ▼ ▼ [ Sympathetic Diaphoresis ] [ Pulmonary Hypoperfusion ] • α1: Skin vasoconstriction • Arterial SpO2 < 88% • M3: Cold sweat surge • Agonal tachypnea (>30 BrPM) • ΔGSR > +3.0 μS/min • Occurs AT REST (SVM < 0.1g)

3. Anatomical Decoupling (2-Node BAN)

Decouples sensing across two physiologically optimal sites without bare-skin chest adhesives:

  • Wrist Slap-Band (Skin Contact): MAX30102 Optical PPG (HR, SpO2) + Grove GSR for palmar sympathetic cold sweats.
  • Sternum Locket (Through-Clothing): MPU6050 Accelerometer coupled to thoracic bone through clothing; measures respiration and fall dynamics.

Deterministic FreeRTOS Allocation: Core 0 manages BLE GATT streaming & GPS NMEA parsing; Core 1 executes DSP ring-buffering & neural inference without packet drops.

5. TinyML Autoencoder & Motion Gating

An unsupervised Autoencoder (8 → 4 → 2 → 4 → 8) models the wearer's healthy resting baseline across 8 normalized physiological features:

x = [HR, HRV-RMSSD, SpO2, Resp-Rate, Resp-Depth, GSR, ΔGSR-Slope, Motion-SVM]

Reconstruction Error: MSE = 1/8 * Σ (x_i - x̂_i)² Kinetic Motion-Gating Formula (Rejects Jogging False Alarms): Threshold_dyn = Threshold_base * [1 + 2.5 * max(0, SVM - 0.40g)] • Exercise (SVM > 0.4g): High HR + High Motion ──► Dynamic Threshold Rises ──► NORMAL • Crisis (SVM < 0.1g): High HR + Cold Sweat + Hypoxia + NO Motion ──► ALARM!
